## Resource investigation

Microsoft's [performance guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/webview2/concepts/performance) recommends Low memory targets for inactive views and restoring Normal for active views. That does not certify the active 150 MiB target. Sharing an environment/UDF can reduce repeated browser-process cost, but this single-session plugin needs isolated per-instance ownership for safe cleanup. Microsoft's [browser-flag guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/webview2/concepts/webview-features-flags) restricts flags such as `disable-gpu` to development/diagnosis and warns against shipping them. No experimental flag has been added to production to declare the budget passing.
